# Design System — LIFE-OS

## Direction
Warm, decorative Soft Academia inspired by the supplied references. The app should feel like a personal journal: polished, warm, playful in small details, but still structured and functional.

## Color tokens
- Bordeaux: #6B2438 — primary actions, active navigation, key emphasis.
- Warm cream: #F6F0E8 — app background.
- Dusty rose: #E6D3D5 — soft card surfaces and calendar accents.
- Mauve: #B99AA7 — secondary accent and charts.
- Sage: #A6AD98 — completed/positive states.
- Dark plum-brown: #3E3034 — primary text.

Use white/cream card surfaces and subtle borders for separation. Check contrast for text and controls; do not use dusty rose or sage as small-text foreground colors unless contrast is verified.

## Typography
- Serif display face for major headings and editorial moments.
- Highly legible sans-serif for body text, forms, labels and numeric data.
- Keep line-height comfortable and labels concise.

## Shape and spacing
- Rounded cards and input fields, consistent spacing scale, gentle shadows only where useful.
- Small decorative details: stars, flowers, bookish motifs, icons, delicate dividers and occasional illustrations.
- Decoration must not interfere with task completion, data density or mobile touch targets.

## Layout
- Desktop: persistent sidebar, top greeting/context, responsive card grid.
- Mobile: compact top bar or bottom navigation; one-column cards; workout entry prioritized.
- Keep primary actions visible and touch targets comfortably sized.
- Provide light mode as the primary theme. Consider dark theme only as a later optional enhancement.

## Component patterns
- Buttons: clear hierarchy, Bordeaux primary, quiet neutral secondary.
- Status: sage for complete, muted rose for due/attention, neutral for inactive.
- Forms: clear labels, units shown explicitly, sensible defaults and validation messages.
- Charts: restrained, accessible colors; always label axes/units and provide a textual summary.
